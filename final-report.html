Previously, on Rook Dispatch

What happened with 4.2

On 12 August, release 4.2 cut the time to answer a job offer from 90 seconds to 60. Heroes missed far more offers. An old scoring rule counted every miss like a "no" and never let anyone climb back. Four heroes sank to the bottom of the list and stopped being asked. Everyone else got their work. Nobody could see why.

The trigger

90s → 60s

Time to answer, cut in one release, alongside a second change.

The surge

22% → 46%

Share of offers missed in release week. All 16 heroes in our sample missed more.

The trap

1 in 4

Heroes effectively cut off: about 12 offers a week down to 0–1.

The pile-up

32% → 48%

Share of all offers going to the four busiest heroes.

Chapter 4 · X-Ray Vision

A miss costs −0.12. A yes earns +0.08. Nothing else gives points back.

It takes three yeses to cover two misses, so a hero has to accept 60% of offers just to stay level. A hero at the bottom gets about one offer a week and rarely catches it. The 60-second clock also starts when the offer is sent, not when it reaches the phone.

My prompt · 04-x-ray-vision #20 Find me the part of this code that takes points off somebody when they miss a ping or turn one down. Show it to me and explain it in plain English. Then find me every single thing in this code that puts points back on. Artifact: history.py traced in synthesis.md · "Points off, and points back on"
The reveal

The root cause

4.2 made heroes miss more offers. A scoring rule from 2019 turned those misses into a trap with no way out. It got worse because two changes shipped at once, with no gradual rollout, no fairness check and no kill switch.

Five investigators agreed

Parallel agents tested five explanations, debated, and aligned at medium confidence.

Ruled out

"August is always soft", availability settings, and the "closer heroes first" change as the trigger.

Still to prove

Late phone delivery and the "no" vs "too late" split. Both need Wen's offer-by-offer log.

Never again

How the skill stops a repeat

What went wrong in 4.2What catches it now
Two changes shipped with no decision record, owner or success measurereview-checklist flags any brief without an owner or "how we'll know it worked"
The headline rate hid four heroesStage 3 · Count must show the rows, raw counts and who gained or lost
"It's seasonal" was accepted without checkingStages 2–3 compare sources and before vs after, and flag every disagreement
A 2019 rule with no way back went unnoticedStage 4 · Trace walks the code or process step by step and names what can't be explained
Silent heroes (Vesper, Meteor Mite) were invisibleStage 2 asks "who never speaks?" Stage 7 tests every role in the flow
Building the wrong thing for the wrong audienceStage 0 · Gate 95% on the ask, the person, the goal and my role before any work
