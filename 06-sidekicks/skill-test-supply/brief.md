# Before 4.3 ships: make urgent gear faster, not slower

**For:** Helen · **From and owner:** Acting Supply PM · **8 Oct 2026** · *Skill test run*

**Who builds it:** the team already committed to 4.3. Its brief names Halloran's team, Supply.

A responder spent **11 days** in the field with a **cracked vest plate** while the replacement
waited for one signature. The 4.3 change we've committed adds a **second** signature to expensive
requests. Plate could be one of them.

## What Halloran lives with today

- **He sets "priority" and nothing changes.** "One queue, everything in it, doesn't matter if
  it's laces or plate."
- **He can't see why it's stuck.** "Takes too long and nobody can tell me why."
- **He reports broken gear and hears nothing back.** "It goes into a void."

## What 4.3 does as planned

It adds a manager's sign-off for anything over $2,000. That answers a fair worry about big
spending. But **it adds a second wait at the slowest step**, with no way to fast-track safety
gear. Its success measure counts sign-offs, not how fast gear arrives.

## What I'd change in 4.3 (same release, same team)

| Keep or add | What it does | Who notices |
|---|---|---|
| **Keep** the second sign-off for big purchases | Still covers spending | Finance and quartermasters |
| **Add an urgent lane** for safety gear, with one signature and a same-day target | Plate doesn't wait behind laces | Halloran, the responder |
| **Add** an alert to the next approver after 48 hours (already in the brief as an extra) | Nothing sits unseen for 11 days | Quartermasters |
| **Add** "where's my request?" for handlers (already in the brief as an extra) | Ends "nobody can tell me why" | Halloran |
| **Add** a link from failure report to replacement: a safety failure makes the replacement urgent automatically | The cracked plate would have jumped the queue | Halloran, the responder |

**Leave for later:** the cross-site dashboard and replacing Halloran's spreadsheet.

## Two cheap checks before we decide (about a week)

1. **Ravi:** do "priority" requests get approved any faster than the rest? If not, the queue is
   ignoring priority.
2. **Me:** talk to 2–3 quartermasters. Do they get alerted to waiting requests, and why do some
   take days?

**What we have today:** one handler's account and no Supply data. These two checks show whether
the 11 days is typical.

## What I need from you

- **Agree to hold 4.3's scope for one week** while we run the two checks.
- **Agree that the urgent lane comes before** the dashboard and spreadsheet work.
