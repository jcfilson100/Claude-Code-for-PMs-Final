    What changes in the reshaped 4.3

    • Safety gear gets an urgent lane: one signature, same-day target.
    • A safety failure report marks the replacement urgent automatically.
    • After 48 hours unseen, a request goes to the next approver.
    • Halloran sees where his request is and who has it.
    • The second sign-off over $2,000 stays, for non-urgent items.

    Illustration, not data

    • The 11-day wait is Halloran's one example. We don't know if it's typical.
    • The plate's cost ($2,400), the queue items and the timings are made up to show the flow.
    • Rook has no Supply data in this repo yet.
