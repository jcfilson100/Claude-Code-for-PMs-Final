How it works

  • Before 4.2 everyone said yes 60%+ of the time, so all heroes start at 1.0.
  • 10 Aug week: real offers and yeses from the chart.
  • After that, offers depend on standing: 0.8+ full · 0.6–0.8 40% · 0.3–0.6 20% · under 0.3 8%. Heroes in full rotation say yes at about their usual rate; heroes who rarely get offers catch only 15% (they're caught off guard).

Check against reality

With today's rules this model reproduces what the chart shows. The same four heroes get stuck (Farlight, Meteor Mite, The Undertow, Vesper), and Mite's offers fall 10 → 4 → 2 → 1, as in the real data.

What it isn't

  • An illustration, not a forecast. The rotation steps are tuned to match the chart, not taken from the code.
  • It doesn't move work between heroes, model distance, or change the 60s answer time.
  • Uses the 16-hero sample (unverified). Wen's replay is the real test.
