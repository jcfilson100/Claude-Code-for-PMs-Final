Standing over time

today's rules with the fix

Standing is the score that decides who gets asked first. At 0.8 or above, a responder is in the full rotation. Below 0.6, they are stuck and almost never get asked.

Wen's two tests

In 2019, Wen left a note in the routing code asking if a low score should ease back over time. She saw both sides:

For: "somebody who had a bad month shouldn't still be carrying it in the spring."
Against: "if somebody has stopped taking work, we probably want that to stick until they take work again."

Wen Li, note in history.py. Nobody came back to it until now.

    Why the fresh start matters

    Recovery on its own doesn't free anyone who is already stuck. They get so few offers that they miss the ones that come, and that cancels out the weekly climb. A fresh start to the middle (0.5) isn't enough either. It has to be a full one. See recovery-simulator.html.

    Where the numbers come from

    • Real: offers and jobs taken for Meteor Mite, Ironvale and The Gale, 3 Aug to 31 Aug, from callout-history.csv. This sample is not yet confirmed by Ravi. The 10 Aug week is real for every responder.
    • Model: everything else, including every standing. An illustration, not a forecast.
    • Guess (behind the scenes only): the model treats 80% of missed offers as "ran out of time" and 20% as a "no". This is a guess. Nobody knows the split until we have the per-offer log, so the phones don't show it.
    • Example: Ironvale's "reached your phone: 1" is made up to show what that check result would look like.

    What it doesn't show

    • It doesn't move work between responders, so it can't show The Gale's load going down.
    • This ships with the answer-time fix (60 seconds counted from when the offer reaches the phone). Without it, heroes can fall back in.
    • "Stopped working" is made up, to run Wen's test.
    • Screens show offer counts and status only, never anything that could identify a responder.
