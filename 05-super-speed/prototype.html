Answering Wen's 2019 question

Wen asked whether a score should "ease back" over time. For: "somebody who had a bad month shouldn't still be carrying it in the spring." Against: "if somebody has stopped taking work, we probably want that to stick."

The proposal does both. A low standing climbs back, but only to the middle, so a bad month fades without putting someone who has stopped working at the front of the queue.

Where the numbers come from

  • Today: real weekly offers for Meteor Mite and The Gale from callout-history.csv (an unverified sample). Standing is estimated from the code's rules.
  • Proposed: an illustration, not a forecast. It assumes most of Mite's release-week misses were "ran out of time".
