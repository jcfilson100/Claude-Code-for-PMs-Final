Why this also helps us

For 7 responders, our data file and the tickets disagree: Nightwell, Stormwrack, Ironvale, Cindermark, Sgt. Falkirk, The Drift and The Longcast. The file shows more offers than they say they get.

If responders could see the same record we see, they would tell us fast when it is wrong. From 10 Aug on, their phones say we are checking their record. We don't call them "busy" or tell them to cut their hours until the check is done.

The "This doesn't match what I see" button lets them tell us. In this prototype it sends nothing.

Where the numbers come from

  • Offers and jobs taken: callout-history.csv, an unverified sample of 16 responders. Not Ravi's official data.
  • Standing: worked out from today's rule. +0.08 for each job taken, −0.12 for each offer not taken, kept between 0 and 1. Shown as a word: In the rotation, Fewer offers, or Stuck.
  • Coming soon: "You're available" and "Your skills were in demand" are greyed out. We have no real data for them yet.
  • Estimate: the days since the last offer is worked out from weekly counts.
  • Reached your phone: unknown until we have Wen's delivery log.

"With the fix" is an illustration

  • It is not a forecast. The four stuck responders (Farlight, Meteor Mite, The Undertow, Vesper) get a fresh start after the 10 Aug week and about their usual offers.
  • The 60 seconds start when the offer reaches the phone. This still needs Wen to confirm.
  • Everyone else is shown unchanged.
  • Privacy: cover names only. No locations. A handler sees only their own responders.
