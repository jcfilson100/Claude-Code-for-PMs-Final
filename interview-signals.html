Rook Dispatch · 4 handler talks + 25 help tickets · Sept 2026

What Handlers and Heroes Told Us

We listened two ways: four long talks with handlers, and 25 help tickets people sent us. Here is what bugs them, sorted into what to fix first, what to fix next, and what can wait.

HandlerThe helper who watches the screen for their hero.
ResponderThe hero who goes out on the job.
CalloutA job offer that pops up on the hero's phone.
ConsoleThe screen the handler watches.
TicketA help message someone sends to Rook.
The big idea

The talks and the tickets agree on three things, and all three are about job offers, not the screen. Some heroes stopped getting jobs. When a job finally comes, it disappears before they can grab it. Nobody can explain why, so people think their account is broken.

The screen problems are real, but nobody sent a ticket about them. So they move down the list.

Remember

Each way of listening misses something. Tickets come from people who feel something is broken. Talks come from four handlers who were asked about the screen. We need both, plus the real numbers.

What only the talks caught

Heroes with too much work. Quiet heroes who never write in. And Dot noticed that the quiet weeks and the vanishing jobs go together.

Good news

Saved filters save Ambrose five minutes a night. Halloran says maintenance scheduling has "gotten smarter." Kip likes seeing two heroes side by side.
