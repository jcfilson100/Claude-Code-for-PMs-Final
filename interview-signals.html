Rook Dispatch · 4 handler interviews · Sept 2026

What the Handlers Told Us

We talked to four handlers. Here is what bugs them, sorted into what to fix first, what to fix next, and what can wait.

HandlerThe helper who watches the screen for their hero.
ResponderThe hero who goes out on the job.
CalloutA job offer that pops up on the hero's phone.
ConsoleThe screen the handler watches.
The big idea

The two worst problems are about callouts, not the screen. Nobody asked about callouts. Three of the four handlers brought them up anyway. Both problems started with the August update (4.2). They are probably one problem with two faces.

Fix first

Heroes are missing jobs

The job disappears before the hero can say yes

The phone buzzes. The hero grabs it. The job is already gone to someone else.

ADHK
3 of 4 said this
"It didn't used to feel like a fair race, phone to stairs, and now it does, and he loses it more than he wins it."Dot

Why first: heroes who lose jobs may stop using Dispatch. In August, the time to answer went from 90 seconds to 60.

Some heroes get almost no jobs. Others get too many.

One hero sits at home all week. Another hero in the same city never stops.

ADHK
2 of 4 said this
"I'm looking at two cards on the same screen that might as well be two different products."Kip

Why first: the quiet heroes feel forgotten, and the busy ones get worn out. Handlers can't explain it, so they have nothing to tell their heroes.

Fix next

The screen is hard to use

The alerts don't tell the handler enough

Handlers want to know the moment a job pops up, and which hero it's for.

ADHK
3 of 4 said this
"Maybe something that tells me too, not just him, when something's come in, so I'm not relying on hearing a buzz through a ceiling."Dot

Why next: better alerts would also help heroes catch jobs before they vanish.

The screen is hard to read

The words are too small, and the screen is too bright at night.

ADHK
3 of 4 said this
"The console is just this wall of white light in the middle of my face."Kip, asking for dark mode

Why next: lots of people want it, and it fits the screen redesign Sofia is already working on.

Later

Small fixes, one person each

1 of 4 · Ambrose
"I'd rather it warned me the filter had reset than simply reset it."

Too much clicking to check on a job

1 of 4 · Ambrose
"More than I'd like, but I don't know that there's a fix for it that doesn't clutter the view for everyone else."

The list of hero skills is hard to find

1 of 4 · Ambrose

Pass to Supply

Not our product, but someone should hear it

Halloran also handles gear, the Supply side of Rook. These belong to the Supply team.

Urgent gear orders wait in the same line as small ones

1 of 4 · Halloran
"One queue, everything in it, doesn't matter if it's laces or plate."
Tell Supply today: a cracked vest plate waited 11 days. That's a safety problem.

Nobody replies to broken-gear reports

1 of 4 · Halloran
"I file a failure report and it goes into a void."

Searching for gear is hard

1 of 4 · Halloran
"If I don't know the exact item name I'm not finding it."

Remember

Four people is a small group. This shows us what to look at, not how big the problem is. The handlers are also telling us what their heroes said, not what they saw themselves.

Good news

Saved filters saved Ambrose five minutes a night. Halloran says maintenance scheduling has "gotten smarter." Kip likes seeing two heroes side by side.
